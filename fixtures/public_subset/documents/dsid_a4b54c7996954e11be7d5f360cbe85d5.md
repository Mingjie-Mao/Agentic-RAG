Streamed per-invocation cost rollups and alert-fuzz controller to reduce noisy long-tail alerts

Motivation: SRE runbooks show a high volume of noisy alert triggers driven by microbursty token-cost spikes from long conversational tails. This PR introduces a streamed rollup pipeline that computes lightweight per-invocation cost summaries (decibel-weighted rollups) in the telemetry ingest path and an alert-fuzz controller that applies hysteresis + probabilistic smoothing to alert evaluation to reduce churn. Key goals: 1) preserve signal for real regressions 2) suppress transient burst noise without dropping visibility 3) keep rollup compute < 2% of telemetry budget. Changes: adds a streaming aggregator service that builds three compact rollup artifacts per invocation (total_tokens, decibel_cost, tail_entropy) and attaches a rollup-hash to the trace; introduces an AlertFuzzController that hooks into the alert pipeline and rewrites thresholds by applying time-decayed fuzz envelopes and cohort-aware suppression; wires rollup metadata into the observability query layer and dashboard panels.
