snapshot-curator-and-playbook-stub-generator

Problem: on-call responders often need a compact, sanitizable snapshot of service state plus a minimal, actionable playbook skeleton to triage quickly. Current incident-bundles are heavyweight and require manual extraction; runbooks stored in the wiki are long and not tailored to the specific alert fingerprint. This PR implements a lightweight 'Snapshot Curator' and a Playbook Stub Generator that produce compact, prioritized diagnostic bundles and tiny playbook skeletons suitable for chat/Slack handoffs and pager messages. Goals: 1) generate a 1-2 screen snapshot that groups key traces/metrics/log-excerpts with sanitized context and concise signal summary; 2) auto-generate a 5-step playbook stub prioritized by probable root-cause signals; 3) provide a safe "preview" mode and redaction hooks for PII-sensitive customers; 4) offer a CLI and Slack action to produce stubs in-line from alerts. Main changes: - new snapshot curator service: heuristics-based selection (error fingerprinting, high-signal metric deltas, recent deployment tags) - playbook-stub generator: maps snapshot features to prioritized actions + suggested queries - telemetry-sampler: adaptive sampling policy (preserves pre/post-deploy windows) to keep snapshots terse but reproducible - CLI: incident-snapshot create --alert-id/--query + --preview flag - Slack action: /snapshot preview (returns ephemeral message with playbook stub) - redaction layer: pluggable redactors and customer opt-out via config - unit+integration tests, updated docs, and small UX flow in incident-bot UI Why this approach: producing smaller, decision-focused artifacts reduces cognitive load and speeds mean-time-to-resolution. The playbook stubs are intentionally conservative (suggest quick probes and safe mitigations) and include an explicit escalation anchor. Backwards compatibility: existing full incident bundles remain unchanged; curator and generator are opt-in behind a feature flag and RBAC scope.
